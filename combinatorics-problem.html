👕👖👟 The Outfit Dilemma

A classic combinatorics problem to test your counting skills.

Problem:
Someone has 4 shirts, 3 pairs of pants, and 2 pairs of shoes. How many different ways are there to wear a set of a shirt, pants, and shoes?
Try the Widget 🧪

The Detailed Solution

To solve this, we need to understand the difference between building a set and just picking an item.

1. The "AND" vs. "OR" Concept

When you get dressed, you must wear a shirt AND pants AND shoes. You are combining items from different categories.

If the problem asked, "You can buy a shirt OR pants," you would just add them (4 + 3 = 7). But because you need a complete outfit, the choices don't just add up—they multiply.

2. Why Multiplication? (The "Repeated Addition" Logic)

Let's ignore the shoes for a moment and just look at the 4 shirts and 3 pants.

Step 1: Mixing Shirts and Pants
Imagine you pick Shirt 1. You can pair it with Pants 1, Pants 2, or Pants 3. That's 3 outfits.
If you pick Shirt 2, you get another 3 outfits.
If you pick Shirt 3, you get another 3 outfits.
If you pick Shirt 4, you get another 3 outfits.

If we add them up, we have: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12.
Multiplication is just a fast way to do repeated addition. So instead of writing 3 four times, we write:
4 shirts × 3 pants = 12 combinations

3. Adding the Shoes

Step 2: Adding the 2 pairs of shoes
Right now, we have 12 different shirt-and-pants combinations.
For every single one of those 12 combinations, you now have 2 choices of shoes: White Sneakers or Black Boots.

So, we have 12 combinations with White Shoes, and 12 combinations with Black Shoes.
Again, repeated addition: 12 + 12 = 24.
As a multiplication shortcut:
12 combinations × 2 shoes = 24 total outfits

4. The Final Answer

By combining the steps, the total number of unique outfits is:

4 × 3 × 2 = 24

💡 The Golden Rule of Counting

AND ➔ Multiply (×)
OR ➔ Add (+)

If you just added 4 + 3 + 2 = 9, you would only be counting the individual items in the closet (9 pieces of clothing). We want to count the combinations of outfits!
